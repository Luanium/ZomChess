# OVERVIEW
- The map is a battlefield containing square tiles.
- Each tile can be one of 6 terrain types (see TERRAIN).
- You control Human to clear all Zombies on the map before the turn limit.
- Human acts first, then Zombies, then the Environment phase.
- Win: eliminate all zombies before the turn limit.
- Lose: your HP reaches 0, or the turn limit is exceeded.

# HUMAN
- White square icon with blinking colorful border.
- Number in the middle = remaining HP.
- Dots near the bottom = remaining Stamina.
## Actions
- Actions of Human take Stamina.
- When running out of Stamina, your turn ends.
- Actions include moving and using weapons or tools.
- Choose which action to be performed via the buttons on the right.
- For most actions, there are directional arrows indicating possible direction choices.
- You can deliberately end Human turn anytime.
- IMPORTANT: You cannot undo actions, so plan carefully before doing anything.
## Stamina
- At the start of each Human turn, Stamina is renewed.
- Stamina can either be rolled randomly from 1 to 6, or be fixed at a value.
- If Paralyzed: Stamina = 0 and Human turn ends automatically.

# WEAPONS AND TOOLS
## Knife
- Melee weapon. Deals 1 damage to an adjacent Zombie.
- Costs 1 Stamina. No ammo required.
## Ice Pick
- Tool to break the Ice into Water.
- Costs 1 Stamina if Human is not Frozen, 2 Stamina otherwise. No ammo required.
## Pistol
- Ranged weapon. Accuracy drops with distance. Deals 1 damage if hit.
- Costs 1 Stamina. -1 ammo each time.
## Shotgun
- Fires in a 3-tile line, hitting all targets in the path. Deals 1 damage each.
- Costs 1 Stamina. -1 ammo each time.
- Effect may be blocked by Wall tiles.
- Firing right at a Wall will destroy that Wall (into Dirt) and Human is knocked back.
## Warp Bolt
- Exchanges 2 tiles: one at the Human-standing tile, the other at the tile the bolt landing.
- The bolt landing on the furthest tile before blocked by Wall.
- Costs 1 Stamina. -1 ammo each time.
- Cause -1HP damage to entities involved in the exchange.
- If the bolt lands on the Human-standing tile, it causes immediate death to Human.
## Grenade
- Thrown randomly 1-6 tiles with a 1-turn fuse.
- Throwing may be blocked by Wall tiles.
- May land on Human's tile and explode immediately if blocked by Wall.
- Costs 1 Stamina. -1 ammo each time.
- AoE: 3x3 if landed on Water or being Frozen under Ice, 5x5 otherwise.
- Damage: center tile -3HP, middle shell -2HP, outer shell -1HP.
- Knock back entities in middle shell.
- Effect may be blocked by Wall tiles.
- Can be blown by Windstorm.
## Molotov
- Thrown randomly 1–6 tiles. Creates a Fire tile on landing.
- Throwing may be blocked by Wall tiles.
- May land on Human's tile if blocked by Wall.
- Costs 1 Stamina. -1 ammo each time.
## Mine
- Placed on the current tile.
- 1-tile blast radius. -1 ammo each time.
- Triggered by: entity steps on it, Lightning, explosion, Fire, Pistol, Shotgun.
- Once exploding, the effects are the same as Grenade.

# ZOMBIE
- Square icons with different colors (see the legends).
- Number in the middle = remaining HP.
- Has tendency to move toward Human.
- Attacks: bite (orthogonal adjacent, -2HP), scratch (diagonal adjacent, -1HP).
- Attacking is the last action in each Zombie's turn.
- Zombie leaves a Loot drop after dying.
## Clever
- 2 HP. Moves 1 tile per turn.
- Can pick up Loot and use Weapons.
- Armed ones are marked with a small black triangle at the icon's top-right corner.
## Fast
- 2 HP. Moves 2 tiles per turn.
- Move 1 tile per turn in Water but can still attack after that.
## Exploding
- 3 HP. Detonates on death, dealing AoE damage. Moves 1 tile per turn.
- AoE: 1x1 if on Water or being Frozen under Ice, 3x3 otherwise.
- Damage when detonate: -1 HP, cause Knocked back.
## Vampire
- 4 HP. Moves 1 tile per turn.
- Steals your HP to heal itself.
## Sick
- 2 HP. Moves 1 tile per turn.
- Biting reduces remaining number of turns by half and -1 HP in next Human turn.
## Corruptor
- 1 HP. Moves 1 tile per turn.
- Can transform adjacent Loot drops into its clones.

# TERRAIN
## Dirt
- Standard tile. Passable.
- May transform into Water or Forest if there is Rain.
## Wall
- Blocks all movement and line-of-sight for ranged weapons.
- Blocks AoE of explosion and Shotgun.
- Can be destroyed into Dirt by explosion and Shotgun.
## Water
- Passable, but costs 2 Stamina to enter.
- Prevent Zombie from attacking after moving (except Fast Zombie).
- Fast Zombie can move only 1 step on Water, but can attack after moving.
- Conducts electricity due to Lightning --> Paralyzed.
- Extinguishes Burned status.
- May become Ice under Blizzard.
- Evaporate into Dirt under Heatwave.
## Forest
- Passable.
- Become Fire if there is an adjacent Fire.
## Ice
- Passable. May cause unintentional sliding.
- Can be broken into Water by Ice Pick.
- Conducts electricity due to Lightning --> Paralyzed.
- Melts into Water under Heatwave.
## Fire
- Temporary, lasts for 2 turns at most then becomes Dirt.
- Cause entities stepping onto it to be Burned.
- Created by Molotov, or when Lightning strikes a Forest tile.
- Cause adjacent Forest to become Fire.
- Extinguished and becomes Dirt under Rain and Blizzard.

# ENVIRONMENT EVENTS
## Clear sky
- Nothing happens.
## Windstorm
- Pushes entities, Grenades and Loot drops 1 tile in the wind direction if not blocked.
## Rain
- Extinguishes all Fire into Dirt and clears Burned status for all entities.
- May transform Dirt into Water or Forest.
## Dark cloud
- Cover the whole map by a black color that lasts until the next Environment phase.
- You can only see Human and the tiles enlightened by Fire.
## Lightning
- Strikes one or more random tiles.
- Entities hit take -1 HP damage.
- Striking on Forest causes Fire.
- Striking on Water or Ice cause spreading of electricity --> Paralyzed.
## Heatwave
- Makes Ice --> Water, Water --> Dirt.
- Kill Forest --> Dirt.
- Speedify conduction of Fire to adjacent Forest.
## Blizzard
- May freeze Water --> Ice.
- Extinguishes Fire --> Dirt and clears Burned status for all entities.

# STATUS
## Burned
- When staying on a Fire tile.
- Loses 1 HP at the end of its turn if Burned is not resolved.
- Resolve: on Water, or if there is Rain or Blizzard.
## Paralyzed
- When staying on Water or Ice which conducting electricity due to Lightning.
- Cannot act for 1 turn.
## Frozen
- When stayin on Water while there is Blizzard that transforms Water --> Ice.
- Cannot move, but can perform other actions.
- Human resolves by using Ice Pick.
- Frozen Loot drops cannot be picked up.

# MECHANISMS
## Knocked back
- Pushed back 1 tile by explosion or Shotgun.
- If blocked, causes Collision damage. 
## Pushed by Windstorm
- Pushed 1 tile in the wind direction if not blocked.
## Stunned
- When sliding on Ice tiles and collision with an obstruction.
- No damage, just end turn immediately.
## Wall-blocking
- AoE of explosions and ranged weapons are blocked by Wall tiles.
## Collision
- If blocked while knocked back, entities in the collision take -1 HP damage each.

# LOOT DROPS
- Zombies drop loot when they die. Stepping on a loot tile to pick it up.
- Multiple zombies dying on the same tile stack their drops.
- Loot frozen under Ice requires an Ice Pick to retrieve.
- Clever Zombie can pick Loot.
- Loot drops can be transformed into Corruptor Zombies.
- Loot is destroyed by Fire, Lightning, explosion and Shotgun.
- Picking a Loot drop returns one of the following possibilities:  
## Loot Types
- Junk: useless.
- PistolAmmo: +3 pistol rounds.
- StaminaPotion: restores Stamina to 6 for Human, or 1 extra turn for Zombie.
- HealthPotion: +2 HP.
- ShotgunAmmo: +1 shotgun shell.
- Grenade: +1 grenade.
- Molotov: +1 molotov cocktail.
- Mine: +1 land mine.
- WarpBolt: +1 warp charge.
