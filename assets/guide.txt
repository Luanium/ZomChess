# TURN STRUCTURE
## Overview
- Human acts first, then Zombies, then the Environment.
- Win: eliminate all zombies before the turn limit.
- Lose: your HP reaches 0, or the turn limit is exceeded.
## Turn Order
- Human phase: spend Stamina to move and use weapons.
- Zombie phase: each zombie takes its action automatically.
- Environment phase: weather events reshape the battlefield.

# STAMINA
## How It Works
- At the start of each human turn, Stamina is rolled: 1–6.
- Moving one tile costs 1 Stamina. Moving onto Water costs 2.
- Each weapon use costs 1 Stamina.
- You cannot move or attack if Stamina reaches 0.
## Stamina Tip
- Plan your moves before spending — you cannot undo actions.
- A Stamina Potion (loot drop) restores Stamina to 6.

# WEAPONS
## Knife
- Melee weapon. Deals 1 damage to an adjacent zombie.
- Costs 1 Stamina. No ammo required.
## Pistol
- Ranged weapon. Accuracy drops with distance.
- Costs 1 Stamina. +3 ammo from PistolAmmo loot drops.
## Shotgun
- Fires in a 3-tile line, hitting all targets in the path.
- Costs 1 Stamina. +1 shell from ShotgunAmmo loot drops.
## Grenade
- Thrown weapon with a 1-turn fuse. AoE blast on detonation.
- Costs 1 Stamina. Found as a loot drop.
## Molotov
- Thrown 1–6 tiles. Creates a fire zone on landing.
- Costs 1 Stamina. Found as a loot drop.
## Mine
- Placed on the current tile. Detonates when a zombie steps on it.
- 1-tile blast radius. Found as a loot drop.
## Warp Bolt
- Teleports the target zombie to a random empty tile.
- Rare loot drop. Costs 1 Stamina.

# ZOMBIE TYPES
## Clever
- Moves 1 tile per turn. Picks up loot and uses weapons against you.
- Most dangerous at range once armed.
## Fast
- Moves 2 tiles per turn. Closes distance quickly.
- Prioritize these early.
## Exploding
- Detonates on death, dealing AoE damage.
- Do not kill at point-blank range.
## Vampire
- Heals HP whenever it damages you.
- Requires sustained damage to bring down.
## Sick
- Infects you on contact, reducing your Stamina next turn.
- Keep your distance.

# TERRAIN
## Dirt
- Standard tile. No movement penalty.
## Wall
- Blocks all movement and line-of-sight for ranged weapons.
## Water
- Passable, but costs 2 Stamina to enter.
- Lightning strikes are more likely on water tiles.
## Forest
- Passable. Blocks line-of-sight — you and zombies cannot shoot through it.
## Fire
- Damages any unit that enters or starts their turn on it.
- Created by Molotov, some environment events.
## Ice
- Passable. May cause sliding — you could overshoot your intended tile.
- Loot frozen under ice requires an Ice Pick to retrieve.

# ENVIRONMENT EVENTS
## Wind
- Pushes units and loot across the board in the wind direction.
## Rain
- Visual effect. May extinguish fire tiles.
## Lightning
- Strikes one or more random tiles (water tiles are higher risk).
- Units hit take damage.
## Heatwave
- Gradually damages units on non-water tiles over time.
## Blizzard
- Freezes water tiles into Ice. Slows movement across the board.

# LOOT DROPS
## How Loot Works
- Zombies drop loot when they die. Walk over a loot tile to pick it up.
- Multiple zombies dying on the same tile stack their drops (shown with a number badge).
- Loot frozen under Ice requires an Ice Pick to retrieve.
## Loot Types
- Junk (75%): useless, but it's something.
- PistolAmmo (5%): +3 pistol rounds.
- StaminaPotion (5%): restores Stamina to 6.
- HealthPotion (4%): +2 HP.
- ShotgunAmmo (4%): +1 shotgun shell.
- Grenade (3%): +1 grenade.
- Molotov (2%): +1 molotov cocktail.
- Mine (1.5%): +1 land mine.
- WarpBolt (0.5%): +1 warp charge — very rare.

# TIPS
## General
- Start on EASY to learn all the mechanics before bumping the difficulty.
- Kill Exploding zombies from a distance — their death blast can hurt you.
- Use Forest tiles for cover against Clever zombies with guns.
## Advanced
- Kite Fast zombies around obstacles to waste their extra move.
- Save Grenades for clustered groups — single-target use is inefficient.
- Stack kills on one turn to maximise multi-kill bonuses.
